# Logos

The uni-helper mark and the project marks. Files are the originals; do not redraw or recolor them.

- `uni-helper-mark.svg` — black heart-and-U mark for light surfaces.
- `uni-helper-mark-inverse.svg` — white heart-and-U mark for dark surfaces.
- `uni-helper-mark-green.svg` — brand-green heart-and-U mark for either surface.
- `uni-helper-favicon.ico` — favicon with 32px and 16px frames.
- `unocss-preset-uni-mark.svg` — three brand-green shapes: pale arch, deep rounded square, green disc.
- `devtools-mark.svg` — green window outline with a three-node graph.
- `desktop-mark.svg` — green window outline with two content lines.
- `create-uni-mark.svg` — green rounded square with a terminal prompt.
- `unocss-applet-mark.svg` — white single-color mark; use a dark ground.

## Rules

- Use the green mark on light and dark surfaces. Use the black mark on light surfaces and the white mark on dark surfaces.
- Keep clear space of at least the height of the U badge on every side.
- Do not add shadows, gradients, or outlines. The marks are single- or two-ink.
- Project marks identify their own project, not the uni-helper organization. Do not use them as the uni-helper logo.
